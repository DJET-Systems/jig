# Code of Conduct

We want jig to be a welcoming, useful project for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for questions, good-faith disagreement, and constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information without
  their permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content, close discussions,
or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe and productive.
If you experience or witness a problem, contact the maintainers privately
through the repository's GitHub contact channels. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
